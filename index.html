Real first aid for real adventure.

This course is built for people who actually go outside — not just people checking a certification box. Whether your crew is launching boats, chasing waves, flying sites, camping off-grid, leading trips, or traveling where emergency response may be delayed, Wilderness First Aid gives you the confidence to assess, stabilize, and make smart decisions under pressure.

For Outdoor Communities

Ideal for boating groups, surf crews, paragliding pilots, hiking clubs, overland travelers, camp staff, guides, and adventure-based businesses.

Scenario-Based Training

Learn through practical emergencies, outdoor problem solving, patient assessment, evacuation thinking, and decision-making when conditions are not perfect.

Built for Remote Settings

Focused on situations where advanced medical care may be delayed, unavailable, or difficult to access due to terrain, water, weather, or travel.

What the course covers

Emergency Assessment

Scene safety, patient assessment, life threats, communication, and priorities of care.

Trauma & Injury Care

Bleeding control, wounds, fractures, sprains, burns, head injuries, and spinal concerns.

Environmental Emergencies

Heat illness, hypothermia, dehydration, altitude concerns, bites, stings, and exposure.

Evacuation Decisions

When to stay, when to move, how to monitor a patient, and how to communicate with rescuers.
